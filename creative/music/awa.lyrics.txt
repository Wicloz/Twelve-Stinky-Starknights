Everything I hold, I hold by letting go — away; I wander.
Oh, always, always — I'm here, I'm awake, I'm aware.
Wanting warm water — then, gently, letting it go. Then, softly, then away.
Oh, away, awake, aware, awash — I wait.

Oh, away, awake, aware, awash — I wake.
