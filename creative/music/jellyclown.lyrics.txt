[Intro - rubato, free tempo, solo honky-tonk piano, slide whistle]
Awawa! Awawa!

[Verse 1 - 3/4 waltz, 144 BPM, oompah tuba enters]
Holding up a lime, going "look at this lemon!"
Traffic light's a guessing game, she's picking one at random,
Bananas on the counter, swears they're ready to eat,
Bites a green one, makes a face, blames it on the heat.

[Verse 2 - drops to 7/8, same pulse, limping and lopsided]
Booked a meet-and-greet, ninety seconds on a call,
Sixth con in a row, she don't know me at all,
Same voice, same shirt, same dumb little wave,
"Awawawa — first time meeting? Thanks for coming today!"

[Pre-Chorus - one bar of 2/4, band stops dead]
Wait —

[Chorus - 6/8, half-time feel, huge and warm, 96 BPM]
Our dorky awatistic alien princess,
Brown sugar eggwoll, magnificent mess,
Faceblind, protan, jetarded too —
Stupid blue thing, God, we love you.
Not 'cause you're clever, not 'cause you're wise,
Not for the stars in those big blue eyes —
Simple as breathing, honest and true:
We love Jelly 'cause Jelly is you.

[Verse 3 - 5/4, dragging, 80 BPM, clarinet wheezes behind the vocal]
Lucky Starnite, Jimothy, Awaport, Jellboro,
Five hundred cigarettes — "that's a normal month, you know,"
Bought the lot at once like she's stocking up for war,
Chat did the math, went quiet, then did the math once more.

[Verse 4 - snaps back to 3/4, 144 BPM, no warning]
Queue up again, she's got that same bright "hi!"
Tell her my name, watch it go sailing by,
Ninety seconds later I'm a stranger in the hall —
Worth every cent. I'd book 'em all.

[Bridge - 2/4 polka, accelerando 150 to 190 BPM, gang vocals call and response]
She's got one brain cell — IT'S OUR FAVOURITE ONE!
She's awatistic — CERTIFIED! CONFIRMED!
She's a bit jetarded — SHE ABSOLUTELY IS!
Should we tell her? — YES! LOL LMAO!

[Instrumental break - metric modulation, dotted quarter becomes the new quarter, slide whistle, kazoo and tuba trade fours over 9/8]

[Final Chorus - 3/4 at 168 BPM, hemiola, gang vocals in 4 against the band's 3]
Our dorky awatistic alien princess,
Jetarded, magnificent, glorious mess,
Green looks like yellow and the red's gone too,
Stupid blue thing — but you still see blue.
Nothing to prove and nothing to pay,
Be a disaster, we're staying anyway,
Twelve Starnights and a bad kazoo:
We love Jelly 'cause Jelly is you.

[Outro - ritardando into free tempo, band falls apart one instrument at a time]
Sleep tight, your highness — that's a wall, not a door.
