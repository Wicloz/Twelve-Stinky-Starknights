[Intro - lone acoustic guitar, single male voice, close and quiet]
They said twelve was not an army.
They said twelve was not enough.
Twelve is a shift.
Twelve is a shift that never ends.

[Verse 1 - male lead, sparse, piano underneath]
We came down on a rock with no name on the chart,
Six sides to every step, and a hex for a heart.
No rifles on our backs, no banner in the wind —
Just a pickaxe and a quota and a place to begin.

They counted us at twelve and they laughed at the odds.
We counted out the tiles and we started to walk.
The furnace took the ore, the ore became the beam,
And the beam became the roof over everyone's dream.

[Pre-Chorus - drums enter, building]
So you can shut your window, you can hold your breath,
You can call us what you like — we have heard it all.
We don't wash, we don't quit, we don't fall behind.
One of us goes down, all twelve are on the line.

[Chorus - lead shouts the call, full choir answers, hands up]
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the flame!)
Twelve pairs of boots and one single name.
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the frame!)
Every hex we touch is never the same.
You can't stop the belt, you can't stop the climb,
The number only goes UP, one more time —
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the light!)
And the whole sky knows it tonight.

[Verse 2 - male lead, driving]
Titanium in the pit and electrum in the vein,
Petrochemicals humming through the pipes in the rain.
Hoshiumium in the dark where the mine goes deep —
We dig for her, and we do not sleep.

Coffee on the farm and coffee in the cup,
Twelve thousand cups if that's what it takes to get up.
A workshop, then a factory, then nine in a row,
Then a suit made of steel with nowhere left to go but GO.

[Pre-Chorus 2 - bigger]
So bring your clean hands, bring your polished shoes,
Bring your empty little planet with nothing to lose.
We'll be here in the morning with grease on our skin,
Twelve stinky Starnights and the whole world within.

[Chorus - lead calls, choir answers, crowd joining]
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the flame!)
Twelve pairs of boots and one single name.
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the frame!)
Every hex we touch is never the same.
You can't stop the belt, you can't stop the climb,
The number only goes UP, one more time —
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the light!)
And the whole sky knows it tonight.

[Bridge - half time, lighters up, choir hums, single voice speaks over it]
Look at it.
That was bare rock. That was nothing.
Now it's a pitmine and an oil rig and a brickworks and a semiconductor factory.
Twelve of us built that. Twelve.
Nobody was born for this. Nobody was special.
That is the whole point.
We just kept showing up.

[Bridge Sung - choir, slow, rising]
One for the ore, and one for the flame,
One for the girl in the stars with the sweetest name.
One for the road and one for the load,
And seven more walking the very same road.

[Key Change - guitars back, everything huge]

[Final Chorus - maximum choir, whole crowd on the answers]
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the flame!)
Twelve pairs of boots and one single name.
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the frame!)
Every hex we touch is never the same.
You can't stop the belt, you can't stop the climb,
The number only goes UP, one more time —
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the weight!)
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry her name!)
WE ARE THE STARNIGHTS! — (we carry the light!)
And the whole sky knows it tonight.

[Outro - choir fading, one voice left, then nothing but a factory hum]
Twelve.
Still twelve.
Always twelve.
Jelly, this one's for you.
